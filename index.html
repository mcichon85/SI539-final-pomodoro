Welcome to your Pomodoro Timer!

This tool is designed to boost your focus and prevent burnout using a simple, proven rhythm.

Focus (25 minutes): Choose a task and work until the timer hits zero.

Rest (5 minutes): Take a quick breather to stretch or grab water.

Recharge (15 minutes): After every four sessions, take a longer break to recharge.
